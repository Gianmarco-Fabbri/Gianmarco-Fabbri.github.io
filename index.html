About Me

I'm an M.Sc. student in Cybersecurity Engineering at Politecnico di Torino. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Bologna, with a thesis on lattice-based post-quantum cryptography.

The path I'm most passionate about is cloud infrastructure and AI security. Right now my goal is to learn in depth the cloud infrastructure that AI systems run on, especially Kubernetes, Docker, and eBPF, and to understand how to apply cybersecurity paradigms to those systems.

Through my university and personal projects I've put into practice Kubernetes runtime security and incident response, real-time systems, and web applications with two-factor authentication and access control. I also interned as a software engineer at My Dev, working on C#/.NET backends and Blazor interfaces.

Current focus: cloud-native infrastructure for AI workloads (Kubernetes, Docker, eBPF) and how security principles such as isolation, least privilege, runtime detection, and incident response apply to AI systems.
